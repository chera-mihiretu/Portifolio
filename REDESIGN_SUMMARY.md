## 🎯 Design Principles Applied

### 1. ✅ ZERO GRADIENTS
- **Replaced all gradients** with solid, bold color blocks
- Used flat colors: `#FFE600` (Yellow), `#0047FF` (Blue), `#FF006E` (Pink), `#00FF94` (Green)
- No blur effects, no gradient backgrounds
- Pure, unapologetic solid colors

### 2. ✅ EXPERIMENTAL TYPOGRAPHY
- **Archivo Black**: Display font for massive headers (900 weight)
- **Space Grotesk**: Technical, geometric font for subheadings
- **DM Sans**: Modern sans-serif for body text (900 weight for emphasis)
- Headers are UPPERCASE and MASSIVE (up to 12rem!)
- Strong visual hierarchy through size and weight contrast

### 3. ✅ LOGO-CENTRIC DESIGN
- **Project logos** oversized (32×32 to 40×40) and placed at creative angles
- **Education logos** displayed as focal points (48×48 to 56×56)
- **Navbar logo** bold with 'C' monogram in brutalist box
- Logos have heavy borders and shadows
- Rotated and positioned asymmetrically

### 4. ✅ CREATIVE EDGE
- **Asymmetric grid layouts** throughout
- **Bold shadows**: 8px, 10px, 12px offsets with solid black
- **Hover effects**: Elements "pop out" with increased shadows
- **Rotation**: Subtle 1-2 degree rotations on cards
- **Overlapping elements**: Background shapes, floating badges
- **Heavy borders**: 4-6px solid black borders everywhere

### 5. ✅ STRICT CONTENT RETENTION
- **All text preserved** - not a single word removed
- **All functionality intact** - buttons, links, navigation
- **All information maintained** - achievements, projects, skills
- Only the **visual shell transformed**

## ✨ Key Design Features

### 1. Oversized Elements
- Logos are 2-3x larger than standard
- Headers dominate the screen
- Icons are bold and prominent

### 2. Bold Shadows
- Every interactive element has shadows

- Alternating left/right alignments
- Overlapping background shapes

### 5. Solid Colors
- No gradients anywhere
- Flat color blocks
- High contrast combinations

### 6. Experimental Typography
- UPPERCASE for emphasis
- Massive font sizes
- Character spacing variations
- Font weight extremes (300-900)
